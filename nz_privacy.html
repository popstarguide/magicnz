MagicNZ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

MagicNZ ("the app", "we", "us") is a New Zealand travel guide app run by [magicnz], an individual developer based in New Zealand. This policy explains what personal information the app collects, why, and what we do with it. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

The short version

We don't like being tracked either, so here it is in plain language: who gets what, and why.

  • Your location stays on your phone. It's used to play commentary and guide you as you travel. It is never sent to us.
  • No Google or Facebook sign-in. You sign up with an email address. Nothing about you is shared with social networks.
  • No ads, no tracking tools, and we never sell your data.
  • The government gets totals only. Tourism agencies may see figures like how many visitors came from Germany, never anything about you personally.
  • Google Maps is the one exception we couldn't avoid. When a map or directions are on screen, Google sees your device and location, and when you enter tonight's accommodation, Google gets that address to plan the last stretch. We don't send Google anything else.
  • You can delete everything. Go to Profile, tap Delete my account, and your details are gone.

The full details are below.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we ask for your full name, your email address and a password, and then a username. Passwords are stored in scrambled (encrypted) form and we can never see them. Your full name and email address are seen only by us. Other travellers see your username.

Travel profile. After you sign up we ask which country you're from, your gender, and who you're travelling with (on your own, or with a partner, family, friends or a tour group). You can leave any of these blank. We use them to understand who visits New Zealand (see "Tourism statistics" below).

Your trip. The route you've chosen, how far along it you are, and the accommodation you've entered are saved to your account, so your trip carries on where you left off if you change phones or log in again.

Location. With your permission, the app uses your phone's location to play commentary, show where you are on your route and give directions. Your location is worked out and used on your phone. It is not sent to us, and we don't keep any history of where you've been. You can turn location off at any time in your phone's settings; the app will still work, but commentary and directions that depend on where you are will not.

Tonight's accommodation. When you plan a day's drive you can enter the name or address of where you're staying. That address is sent to Google Maps to plan the last stretch of the day's route.

Forum posts. If you post in the forum, your post, your username and your country can be seen by other signed-in MagicNZ users. Only share what you're happy for other travellers to read. You can delete your own posts at any time.

Purchases. MagicNZ is free while it is being tested. If paid content is added, it will be sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment, and we never see or store your card details. We would receive only confirmation of what you've bought, so we can unlock it in your account.

What we don't collect. The app doesn't use your microphone, camera, contacts or photos, and it has no advertising or analytics tools in it.

How we use your information

  • To create and look after your account, and keep your place on your trip
  • To play commentary and give travel information and directions for where you are
  • To improve the app and what's in it
  • To produce anonymous tourism statistics (see below)
  • To reply to you if you contact us

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Tourism statistics

When you fill in your travel profile, your country, gender, who you're travelling with and the month you joined are copied into a separate statistics record. That record holds no name, no email address and no link back to your account, so it can't be traced to you. We may share combined totals from these records, such as the number of visitors from a particular country over the past year, with New Zealand government agencies and tourism organisations to support tourism research.

Other companies involved

We keep outside services to a bare minimum. Here is every one of them and what it gets:

  • Supabase stores accounts, saved trips and forum posts on servers in Sydney, Australia, and sends the emails that confirm your address or reset your password. Australian privacy law gives protections comparable to New Zealand's.
  • Google Maps draws the maps and works out the directions. To be honest, we'd rather not use Google, but there is no practical alternative for maps of this quality. Whenever a map or directions are on screen, Google receives your device details and location, and handles them under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy), which we have no control over. When you enter tonight's accommodation, that address is also sent to Google to plan the last stretch of the drive. We don't pass Google anything else about you.
  • GitHub hosts the app's photos. When photos load, GitHub sees your phone's internet (IP) address, as any website would. It receives nothing else about you.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play deliver the app, and would handle any purchases, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your name, email address, username, travel profile, saved trip and forum posts are removed straight away, and are gone from any backups within 30 days. The anonymous statistics record described above remains, because it was never connected to you.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. You can change your profile, or delete your account, at any time from the Profile screen in the app. If you can't get into the app, email us and we'll delete your account for you.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have the same rights under the data protection law there: to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your information, and to complain to the data protection authority in your own country.

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first. If we can't sort it out, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at www.privacy.org.nz.

Children

MagicNZ is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change is a big one, we'll tell you in the app.

Maori Legends

This collection it has to be noted are a few I have chosen because of their significance to the locations they are centred about or set the scene for further understanding of culture. A high number of legends have different versions depending on the iwi or person telling them. These have been sourced from “Reed book of Maori mythology”. In the writing of this book I have tried to find the accepted version of these legends. I also have tried to honour these stories and their places through sharing them with you.

The Maori made sense of their surrounding and retold their history through legend for hundreds of years. What’s more the legends are usually more engaging than the scientific stories.

Guide to the App

How to get started

This app is designed to be used before you travel and are in planning stages as well as while you travel.

I have built this app to be your own tour guide in your device, a guide that will give commentary for towns you visit. Also to be a resource you can use for research.

Start

Before you do anything, work out how much time you have to see New Zealand.

Then use the “Routes” section to determine the best option for you, which route covers the parts you want to experience in the time you have. I have suggested four routes.

Make sure you explore the shortcuts if time is important, equally these can be left in your back pocket in case you need to get home quicker than planned.

I have also indicated how long in minimum nights it should take you to do each route.

Use the “Recommendations” and “Travelling Tips” sections to help you prepare your journey. These sections cover: accommodation advice, niche road rules of NZ, important dates that may impact your travels.

Getting close to travelling

I recommend you read the “NZ in Summary” section, especially “Environment”. These will give you a background to New Zealand, its culture, and history.

Travelling around

Once you have worked out which Route you are taking, select the route from the menu page (car icon) and follow its navigation and commentary.

The “Locations” section is a guide by town. I suggest you leave this section in the background for when you need to look up a specific location, for example if someone mentions a town, use it to look up that town.

What you are about to discover is our lush rolling countryside, beaches, mountains, cities, history, and culture.

Kiwi lingo & Te Reo

As you travel you will hear plenty of Te Reo Māori, most of our place names are Māori, and us Kiwis have a fair bit of slang of our own. Don’t let that put you off, give it a go, the locals will appreciate you trying.

The “NZ Dictionary” (book icon) has Māori and Kiwi words and phrases, plus a guide on how to pronounce Māori words.

While you are reading, if you come across a word you don’t know, press and hold on it and its meaning will pop up. Give it a go on the word Kiwi.

Kia ora – hello, thank you and welcome, all in one.
